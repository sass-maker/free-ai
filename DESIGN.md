# Design

## Direction

Preserve the AI Gateway’s dark operator-console identity: near-black blue surfaces, cyan and emerald signals, DM Sans body copy, Instrument Serif display type, and precise status-oriented borders.

## Rules

- Public reading surfaces inherit the existing landing-page palette and typography.
- Cyan identifies navigation and primary project actions; emerald identifies verified metadata.
- Keep technical content direct, spacious, and free of decorative dashboard chrome.
- Mobile layouts may wrap navigation but must not clip it.
